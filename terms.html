Terms of Use

Last updated: 7 October 2026

WonderWorld+ is not on sale yet. The subscription screens exist in the app, but no payment can be taken and no subscription can be started. This page describes how it will work, and will be finalised before anything goes on sale.

The core WonderWorld adventure is free. Educational progress is earned through learning — never through a purchase.

1. What WonderWorld is

WonderWorld is an educational game for children of roughly 5 to 12. It is intended to be used by a child with a grown-up's knowledge and permission. It is a game, not a curriculum, an assessment, or a substitute for school.

2. The free adventure

These are free, and we do not intend to charge for them:

  • Creating an Explorer and choosing a companion
  • Math Island, Story Forest, Science Lab, Planet City and Business Town
  • All five Knowledge Crystals and the complete Knowledge Tree adventure
  • XP, gems, levels and badges
  • One Explorer profile, saved on the device
  • The progress summary for grown-ups
  • Every accessibility setting, and the read-aloud and audio features

Worlds open when the child has earned enough XP, or restored enough crystals. That is the only way they open. A subscription does not and will not change it.

3. WonderWorld+

WonderWorld+ is an optional subscription that adds content and family features on top of the free adventure. It is a growing collection rather than a shop: there is nothing to buy individually.

4. Payment never replaces learning

Some content has both a learning requirement and a subscription requirement. Both must be met. WonderSpace, for example, needs all five Knowledge Crystals restored and WonderWorld+. A subscriber who has not earned the crystals cannot open it.

We do not sell, and will not sell: XP, gems, crystals, answers, hints, lives, energy, randomised rewards, or any form of advantage in a learning activity.

5. Prices, trials and cancelling

  • Prices shown in the app are in US dollars and may differ in other regions.
  • A free trial, where offered, converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless it is cancelled before then.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future charges.
  • When a subscription ends, WonderWorld+ content closes — but the free adventure and all of your child's saved progress remain exactly as they were. We do not delete a child's progress because a subscription lapsed.

When WonderWorld+ is sold through an app store, that store handles the payment, the renewal, the cancellation and any refund, under that store's own terms. Cancellation is done in the device's account settings, not here.

6. Children and purchases

Nothing in the child's game shows a price or asks for money. Subscription information sits behind an adult check, and the child-facing message when they reach WonderWorld+ content simply asks them to fetch a grown-up. Purchases are for the account holder to make, and we ask that a device used by a child has the store's own purchase restrictions enabled.

7. Your child's data

Progress is stored on the device and is not uploaded. We do not require an account or a child's email address, and there are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no chat and no social features. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

8. Acceptable use

9. The game is provided as it is

WonderWorld is offered on an "as is" basis. We work hard to keep it correct and available, but we cannot promise it will be free of faults or always reachable. Nothing here limits any right you have that the law where you live does not allow to be limited — including consumer rights around refunds and subscriptions.

10. Changes

We may update these terms as the game changes. Material changes affecting a paid subscription will be communicated before they take effect, and the date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
